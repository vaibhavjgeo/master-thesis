# Climate Change × Shallow Geothermal Potential (M.Sc. Thesis, KIT)

Interactive results of my Master's thesis: a Python framework coupling CMIP6 climate projections
(8 GCMs, SSP 2-4.5 & SSP 5-8.5, processed in Google Earth Engine) with finite-line-source borehole
heat exchanger theory across Germany at 5 km resolution.

Live: https://vaibhavjgeo.vercel.app/thesis · Fullscreen atlas: https://vaibhavjgeo.vercel.app/thesis/map-fullscreen.html
